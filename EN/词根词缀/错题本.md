
The university has implemented strict plagiarism‑detection software to ____ academic integrity across all departments.

**A: preserve**
B: conserve
C: reserve
D: deserve

plagiarism n. 剽窃，抄袭；剽窃作品
**preserve**
维护、保全（抽象的价值 / 原则 / 秩序），搭配 preserve academic integrity = 维护学术诚信，符合本句语境。
conserve
节约、保护（资源、能源，强调减少损耗），例：conserve water 节约用水，不用于抽象道德准则。
